# earth, lately.

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the repository with `python3 -m http.server`.
The checked-in `app.js` is a classic-script bundle, so the face works on `file://`. Live APIs require internet access and source CORS support.

The blue Three.js globe has matte features, locally bundled Natural Earth v5.1.2 land polygons and a temperature atmosphere. Continents use a clean sage fill, a subtle coastline border and broad cartoon shading, without noisy terrain or bump relief. No body, ears, hair, surface markers or camera features remain. A locally drawn SVG gives the dark navy background cartoon stars, dashed orbital paths and an outlined ringed planet; decorative art does not intercept globe gestures.

Drag the globe horizontally through 360° and vertically toward either pole. Pointer capture supports mouse, pen and touch. A drag does not play the voice. Double-click (double-tap on touch) a location to inspect its coordinates, current weather and air quality; a single stationary click plays the voice after a short gesture-disambiguation delay. Use the reset button or Home to return to the front; arrow keys turn the focused globe and I inspects the centre point. The entire face and map rotate together. Pointer gaze, API-driven quake shaking, tears and temperature expressions remain active. Reduced-motion preferences stop automatic motion, while manual rotation remains available.

The Air button opens city search and local air quality. Open-Meteo geocoding resolves names, and its air-quality API supplies current US AQI and PM2.5 model estimates from CAMS. New York is the initial example, not inferred device location. Your chosen city and query persist in localStorage; no geolocation permission is requested. Checks run every 15 minutes. Values show the city and model-valid UTC timestamp; failed or more-than-two-hour-old data adds no air expression. Above AQI 50, the eyes become progressively heavier toward AQI 200. This local reading does not replace the global climate sources. Search and reading requests reject outdated responses when a newer city or query is selected. Sources:
- https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api
- https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api

Temperature: NASA GISTEMP v4 monthly global land–ocean anomalies versus 1951–1980. The dated bundled reading is September 2026, +1.38°C; August was +1.42°C. Positive anomalies thicken a yellow/orange/red rim; negative anomalies add a blue/icy rim and surface frost. Both reach their strongest artistic stage at ±1.5°C. A positive current reading does not artificially freeze.

Sea level: NASA Earth Indicator, +115.8 ±4.0 mm since 1993, October 2026. These dated climate observations were checked against their original sources on November 5, 2026. Refresh is attempted daily; dates remain visible if direct access fails.

Rain: Open-Meteo current-model precipitation at 36 evenly spaced latitude/longitude points, area-weighted and normalized to mm/hour. It is a coarse estimate, not an official measured global mean. Rain and sea level drive water pooling, continuous flowing streams and sobbing. Stream width, length, speed and opacity increase together.

Quakes: USGS past-day catalog. Counts in adjacent one-hour windows drive head-shake speed, angle and burst duration. Old data is not treated as a current increase. Quakes are a separate geological feed, not a climate-change indicator. No epicentre markers or CCTV requests are used.

Click the globe or press Enter/Space when focused to hear a synthesized “Aaaah!” and open the mouth briefly. The sound button controls automatic audio. Click the speech bubble to cycle through phrases related to the actual active reactions. The ivory speech bubble uses one continuous rounded contour. It sits bottom-right with a left tail on desktop; at 950px and below it occupies a separate row above the portrait with a bottom tail, avoiding overlap with the face. Outlined pastel buttons, illustrated metric cards and cream popups match the cartoon face against the illustrated navy background. Source explanations and raw JSON use native expandable sections. Retained dates and unavailable-feed labels stay visible. No external fonts or decorative image assets are required.

Edit `script.js`, `climate.js`, `globe.js`, `orbit.js`, `air.js`, `air-data.js`, `sound.js`, `style.css`, and `index.html`. Rebuild JavaScript using an esbuild installation outside the repository:

```sh
esbuild week11/script.js --bundle --format=iife --target=es2020 --minify --legal-comments=linked --outfile=week11/app.js
```

Run checks with `node --test week11/tests/*.test.mjs`.
Three.js retains `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`; Natural Earth provenance and public-domain terms are in `assets/NATURAL-EARTH.txt`. Class exercises remain independent.


The conditions panel is up to 800px wide. Point weather and air quality sit side by side above 700px; smaller screens stack compact cards. Selected location, actual values, model times and failures stay visible. Search and saved places share one folded tool; the Air header button opens it, while a globe selection closes it and returns the panel to the top. A single folded API inspector contains the actual returned JSON, request URL, HTTP status, browser receive time and shared expression rule. These are separate timestamps; receiving a response does not imply that the model values changed. Failed or invalid responses clear the previous city's reading and expression.

Save up to six cities in `earth-favorite-cities`. The array is validated and deduplicated when restored. Saved chips select a city and fetch current data; no air readings are persisted as live values. Remove controls edit the same array and persist it. If storage is unavailable, feedback says that changes are for the current visit only.

`searchCities()` and `fetchAirQuality()` handle API access in `air-data.js`; `getAirMood()` supplies the exact same intensity and thresholds to the UI and renderer. `updateEarthFace()`, `saveFavoriteCity()` and `renderFavoriteCities()` make the event/array/DOM/storage flow explicit in `air.js`. Globe point inspection: `globe-input.js` maps the UV coordinates returned by a raycast against the head to latitude/longitude. Because Three.js handles the nested world transforms, this works after yaw, pitch, pointer tilt and API shaking. Selection uses the land texture’s actual geographic layout; no permanent dots or raised markers are added. Mouse double-click, touch double-tap and keyboard I select a point. Dragged or cancelled gestures never become inspection requests. The single-click voice waits 600 ms on mouse or 350 ms on touch, and audio is prepared silently in the original gesture before delayed playback. Hidden-page pending clicks are cancelled.

`point-data.js` requests Open-Meteo current temperature, precipitation and wind at the selected coordinates (`cell_selection=nearest`). Precipitation is converted from its interval amount to mm/hour. The same conditions panel shows model-valid/received times, source request URLs and actual JSON. Weather and air requests are independent; either can fail without hiding the other. Local weather does not override the global temperature anomaly or global mean rain estimate. The location heading shows country, region and locality above the exact selected coordinates. For land points, Photon reverse geocoding supplies nearby OpenStreetMap place names in English where available. It does not move the selected coordinates or the weather/air request to the returned feature. Ocean points show Ocean and skip reverse lookup; unnamed or unavailable places retain their coordinates. Lookup failure does not hide weather or air data. The same six-place favorite array supports cities and coordinate points; point mode and exact coordinates survive a reload. Source: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs

Ocean double-clicks: `surface.js` tests the selected coordinates against the same bundled land polygons used to paint the globe. Open ocean displays an English ocean story and the dated global sea-level observation. Land and inland polygon holes do not trigger the ocean story. The quoted rise describes the global record since 1993, not a local tide or a new measurement at the clicked point. Without a positive sea-level observation, the story avoids claiming rising water. Closing the panel keeps the ocean thought in the main speech bubble briefly; clicking the bubble cycles related lines. Saved points retain their surface type. New globe inspections reset the popup scroll position so the selected context is visible.

Place names: `location-data.js` calls Photon only for selected points without stored place metadata. Successful results are cached for up to 50 coordinates during the visit; saved points retain validated country/region/locality metadata without changing their favorite identity. Lookup runs independently of weather and air and rejects responses belonging to earlier selections. Building/landmark names are excluded from the administrative label. A 20 km reverse-search radius can return a nearby mapped place, so names are approximate labels rather than administrative boundary guarantees. API and usage: https://github.com/komoot/photon/blob/master/docs/api-v1.md and https://github.com/komoot/photon#demo-server.
